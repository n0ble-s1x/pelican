The fjord, three ways

One world in all three: cold blue-black ground drawn from fjord depth, an absolute waterline as the only structural rule, and a single warm value — low sun on water, brass rather than orange — reserved for the active state and the primary action. The discipline borrowed from Scandinavian design is restraint and craft in the joinery, not ornament: no applied decoration anywhere, and nothing on screen that is not doing work. What differs is structure.
